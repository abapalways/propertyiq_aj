Agentic AI Project Demo Day

PropertyIQ

A compliance-aware property search agent
01 / 08
1 min Situation

Who this is for, and what it replaces

  • Real estate buyers searching listings by structured criteria (price, bedrooms, garage) and fuzzy, qualitative criteria (good schools, move-in ready)
  • A chat agent replaces manual portal filtering — plus mortgage estimation, comps lookup, and cross-session memory of rejected listings
02 / 08
1 min Complication

Today, one decision means many destinations

  • Without an integrated agent, a buyer searches listings on one site or portal
  • Then goes somewhere else entirely to calculate a mortgage payment
  • Then somewhere else again for comps, or other integrations layered on separately
  • Every hop is a place the buyer can lose momentum — or leave
03 / 08
1 min Implication

What fragmentation costs the business

  • Lost revenue — buyers drop off when there's no one-stop shop for their whole decision
  • Dissatisfaction — repeated context-switching across tools erodes trust in the experience
  • A buyer who leaves to check a mortgage calculator elsewhere may not come back
04 / 08
3 min Position — Our Solution

Retrieval and truthfulness, as two separate steps

05 / 08
3 min Position — Beyond Search

Tools, guardrails, memory, evals

  • Tool calling: search, mortgage calc, comps lookup, rejection memory — with dedup guards against redundant repeated calls
  • Guardrails: fair-housing check runs before search — a blocked request never touches the vector store
  • Memory: rejected listings persist across sessions — verified across a real process restart
  • Evals: assertion-based suite + LLM-as-judge groundedness check — two complementary types
  • Observability: full LangSmith tracing, every call

2 min Learnings

What this project actually taught us

  • Search is genuinely harder than it looks — pushed past the first "good enough" answer multiple times to find an architecture that actually held up under real testing
  • Guardrails and memory were new territory — first hands-on implementation of compliance enforcement and cross-session persistence in an agentic system
  • Prompt engineering was constant, front-line work — continuously distilling and filtering down to only the information the model actually needed, at every layer of the pipeline
  • Built the discipline to test a fix against a second, independent check before trusting it — not just the first eval that passes
